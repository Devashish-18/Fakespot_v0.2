How Our AI Works
Features Analyzed:
  • Profile Picture: Presence of profile image
  • Username Pattern: Digit ratio and complexity
  • Full Name: Word count and digit ratio
  • Name Match: Username vs full name similarity
  • Bio Description: Length and presence
  • External URL: Presence of external links
  • Privacy Settings: Account privacy status
Engagement Metrics:
  • Total Posts: Number of posts published
  • Followers: Number of followers
  • Following: Number of accounts followed
  • Follower/Following Ratio: Engagement pattern analysis
  • Statistical Analysis: Deviation from normal patterns
  • Machine Learning Models: Ensemble prediction
  • Confidence Score: Prediction reliability
Detection Process: The AI analyzes multiple account characteristics and compares them against patterns learned from thousands of real and fake Instagram accounts. Using Random Forest and Decision Tree algorithms, it calculates a confidence score indicating the likelihood of an account being fake or real.
